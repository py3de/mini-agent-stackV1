# Contracts and pricing

## Contract process
After budget approval we send the contract and the DPA together. The customer names an authorised
signatory; contracts are signed electronically. Project kickoff is scheduled within 10 working days
after signature.

## Payment terms
Fixed-price projects are invoiced 40% at signature, 40% at pilot start and 20% at go-live.
Payment terms are 30 days.

## Changes after signature
Scope changes are handled with a change request that needs written approval from both sides.
