# Services

## CRM agent rollout
We build AI agents on top of the customer's HubSpot CRM: deal summaries, meeting-notes-to-CRM agents
and follow-up email drafting. A typical rollout takes 6 to 8 weeks: 2 weeks discovery, 3 to 4 weeks
build, 1 to 2 weeks pilot with one sales team.

## Service Hub migration
Migration of support tickets, knowledge base and workflows from another helpdesk to HubSpot Service Hub.
Typical duration is 4 to 6 weeks, depending on the number of workflows.

## Sales copilot
An assistant inside HubSpot that answers questions about accounts and deals and suggests next steps.
Usually starts with a 2-week proof of concept on the customer's own data.
